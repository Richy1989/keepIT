A fast, private notes app. Your notes live on your phone, and on your own
server if you want one - never on ours.

Open it and start writing. No account, no setup, no connection: everything is
stored on the device first, so keepIT is instant and works offline. When you
want the same notes on your other devices, run the free and open-source keepIT
server and they sync in real time. You can start without one and connect a
server later - your notes come with you.

* Notes and checklists, with Markdown as you type
* Voice notes - record one and play it back, straight from your list
* Photos from your camera or gallery
* Reminders that fire on time, offline and with the app closed
* Colors, pins, lists, archive and search to keep it findable
* Share a note with someone to view or edit (needs a server)
* Home-screen widget, and text shared in from other apps

No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
